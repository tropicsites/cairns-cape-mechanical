Knowledge earned in the real world.

At Cairns and Cape Mechanical, experience meets reliability. With more than 25 years in the industry, we specialise in servicing and repairing outboards, PWC, motorcycles and a wide range of machinery.

We are proud to be an authorised Tohatsu Marine dealer, offering quality products alongside expert support.

Our OEM training spans leading brands including Yamaha marine, Yamaha PWC, Yamaha motorcycles, Husqvarna, Kawasaki, Can-Am, Sea-Doo, Caterpillar and Hitachi machinery.

This means every job is approached with a broad base of practical experience rather than a one-size-fits-all solution.
