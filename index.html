About the business

Knowledge earned in the real world.

Cairns and Cape Mechanical was built around a simple idea: good mechanical work should leave you confident about what happens after you leave the workshop.

With more than 25 years in the industry, our experience spans marine, PWC, motorcycles and machinery. That includes OEM training with Yamaha, Husqvarna, Kawasaki, Can-Am, Sea-Doo, Caterpillar and Hitachi.

Growing up around the water and working across Cape York and Far North Queensland means we understand the environments this equipment is actually used in — not just the workshop manual.
