About Us

Experience You Can Trust

At Cairns & Cape Mechanical, experience isn’t just measured in years — it comes from a lifetime spent on the water, riding & racing motorcycles, operating and working on machinery in Cape York & Far North Queensland.

With over 25 years of hands-on mechanical experience, including Yamaha OEM factory trained technician, we bring extensive knowledge and factory training across Yamaha, Can-Am, Kawasaki and Husqvarna, specialising in Marine, PWC and Motorcycle servicing, diagnostics and repairs.

We pride ourselves on quality workmanship, accurate diagnostics, honest advice and dependable service. It’s not just about fixing your equipment — it’s about keeping it reliable and giving you confidence every time you turn the key.
