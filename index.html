About Cairns & Cape Mechanical

Local knowledge.
Serious experience.

Built around reliability.

At Cairns and Cape Mechanical, experience meets reliability. With over 25 years in the industry, we specialise in servicing and repairing outboards, PWC, motorcycles and a wide range of machinery.

Our OEM training spans leading brands including Yamaha marine, PWC and motorcycles, Husqvarna, Kawasaki motorcycles, Can-Am quads and side-by-sides, Sea-Doo personal watercraft, Caterpillar and Hitachi machinery.

We are a fully mobile mechanic service covering a vast region from Ingham all the way to the tip of Cape York. We also have a fully equipped workshop for the bigger jobs.

Reliable, experienced and ready to travel — Cairns & Cape Mechanical is your trusted partner in the field.
